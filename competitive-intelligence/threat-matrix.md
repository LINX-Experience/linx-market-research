## Strategic Summary — Nov 7, 2026

**No competitor has built trust-verified peer referral attribution for luxury travel. This moat remains intact.** But the competitive pressure has escalated materially since April 2026.

### What Changed (Apr → Nov 2026)
- **Fora**: Most dramatic escalation. $78.5M → $138.5M. Pre-unicorn → $1B. This is not incremental. At unicorn scale, Fora has the runway to build the peer layer if they ever choose to. Watch Via's product roadmap obsessively.
- **Mindtrip**: Closed the "no booking" weakness by launching Mindtrip Flights with full transaction ownership (Sabre+PayPal). No longer just an itinerary tool.
- **Virtuoso**: Launched actual AI capability — first real tech move in 40 years. Still not a peer layer, but signals the incumbents are waking up.

### What Hasn't Changed (LINX's enduring advantages)
- Fora: 15,000 strangers selling trips. Trust cannot be manufactured at scale.
- Virtuoso: AI tool helps advisors research. Zero peer-to-peer mechanism.
- Mindtrip: Books flights. Cannot verify who to trust for a $200K expedition charter.
- TEC: 24K HNW members who recommend to each other constantly — zero of it tracked or rewarded.

### Headline Assessment
The moat is real. The clock is faster. LINX must have a revenue-generating operator on the network before Fora launches any peer or social feature. Every month without revenue is a month closer to losing first-mover advantage on trust infrastructure.
