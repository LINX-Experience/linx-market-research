## Planning Fees (Advisor-Charged — Not LINX)

For context, luxury advisors increasingly charge separate planning fees:
- Domestic trip: $250 flat
- International leisure: $500–$1,000
- Multi-destination/complex: $1,500–$3,000
- Flight-only service: $25–$175/ticket

**LINX implication:** LINX referrers earn 10% of the booking value with zero planning work. At $50K booking, referrer earns $5,000. An advisor charging $1,000 for planning + 15–18% commission earns ~$9,500 but spends 20+ hours building the itinerary. LINX referrers earn $5,000 for one trusted recommendation. Zero overhead.

## Key Insight for Operator Pitches

The arbitrage is largest in:
1. **Safari lodges** (10–15 points saved)
2. **Yacht charter** (10–15 points saved)
3. **Expedition cruise** (5–8 points saved)
4. **Luxury hotels** (7–10 points saved on preferred)

Lead with safari and yacht in operator conversations. These operators have the most to gain.

## Affiliate / Referral Program Benchmarks

For comparison with LINX's model:
- Travel affiliate programs: 4–6% (OTAs) to 5–15% (luxury programs)
- Fixed referral fees: $150–$5,000 depending on booking size
- LINX at 10% flat is above OTA affiliate rates and competitive with luxury referral programs — but LINX adds trust verification, attribution, and CRM integration that raw affiliates do not.
