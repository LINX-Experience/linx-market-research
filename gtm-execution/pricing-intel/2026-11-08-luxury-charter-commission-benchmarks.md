## Superyacht Charter Market: Current State

### Fleet & Availability
- **Active charter fleet:** ~1,500 vessels above 24m globally (Mare Forum estimate)
- **FLIBS 2026 charter inventory:** 60 yachts available for charter out of 211 attending = **28.44% charter share**
  - Down from 35.29% in 2025
  - Absolute charter count unchanged; fleet growth outpaced charter availability
  - **Implication:** Charter is becoming relatively scarcer as more owners keep boats for private use

### Market Size
- **Luxury yacht market:** Projected to exceed $15B by 2032 (Mordor Intelligence, Nov 5 2026)
- **Growth driver:** Demand strongest for mid-sized yachts; West Mediterranean leads destination preference
- **Charter tightening:** Operating costs rising, pushing some owners out of charter program

## Commission Rate Intelligence

### Industry Standard (Yacht Charter)
| Broker Type | Commission Range | Notes |
|-------------|-----------------|-------|
| Charter broker (standard) | 15–20% of charter fee | Industry standard for traditional brokers |
| Charter broker (high-volume) | 12–15% | Volume discounts rarely passed to clients |
| LINX model | **5% of charter fee** | CONFIRMED vs industry 15–20% = **operator saves 10–15 points** |

**Arbitrage argument for operator pitch:** "A $500K/week charter at Burgess pays $75–100K in broker fees. LINX delivers the same booking at $25K. The 10–15 point difference is fully retainable by the operator OR passed to the charterer as pricing advantage."

### Advisory Commission (Luxury Travel, Non-Yacht)
| Advisor Type | Commission Range | Notes |
|--------------|-----------------|-------|
| Virtuoso advisor | 10–15% | On qualifying bookings through network |
| Fora advisor | 10–20% | Standard varies by operator agreement |
| Independent advisor | 8–20% | Wide range |
| LINX curator/referrer | **10%** | Flat rate for peer referrals (non-yacht) |

**Key insight:** LINX is at the bottom of the advisory range (10% flat) but with one structural advantage — no advisor overhead. A Fora advisor provides planning, service, and logistics. A LINX referrer just makes the introduction. Operators paying 10% for LINX receive a lead, not a managed booking. The question the ILTM pitch must answer: how do operators value a trust-verified lead vs. a fully-managed booking?

## Bundled Luxury Pricing (New Trend)

### Fraser + Wheels Up Partnership (Aug 12, 2026)
- **Model:** Preferred charter rates for Wheels Up members; combined jet+yacht itineraries
- **What it signals:** The luxury buyer's willingness-to-pay is for the full journey, not just the vessel
- **Pricing implication for LINX:** A LINX referral that includes "how to get there" (aviation recommendation) is worth more to the operator than a booking-only referral
- **Action:** Consider whether LINX's referral layer should cover aviation recommendations to same operators, or just surface aviation partner contacts
