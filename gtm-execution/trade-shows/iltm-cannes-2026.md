## Show Overview

The International Luxury Travel Market (ILTM) Cannes is the flagship global B2B luxury travel event. 3,000+ exhibiting companies from 100+ countries. 85,000+ pre-scheduled 1:1 appointments across 4 days. Format: curated buyer-seller appointments (pre-scheduled; walk-up networking supplementary). Full vetting of attendees. No mass-market presence.

**Who attends:**
- Ultra-luxury operators (lodges, expedition cruise, yacht charter, private island resorts)
- Luxury travel advisors / independent curators (LINX's ideal curator pipeline)

- Luxury DMCs and destination marketing organizations

**Who exhibits:**

- Expedition and adventure operators
