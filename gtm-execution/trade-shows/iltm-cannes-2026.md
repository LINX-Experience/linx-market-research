ILTM is the highest-density luxury travel B2B event globally. Every luxury travel advisor, tour operator, DMC, hotel group, and media property that LINX needs to reach attends here. The 2026 format is built around 85,000 pre-scheduled one-to-one meetings — the most structured prospecting environment in the industry.

**What LINX can accomplish at ILTM:**
- Recruit luxury travel advisors as LINX curators/referrers (the network side of the platform)
- Pitch operators for Wave 1–3 partnerships (the supply side)
- Establish LINX in the minds of the advisor community before Fora's consumer pivot lands in 2027
- Gather competitive intelligence on Virtuoso, Fora presence, and new entrants

**What is at stake:** Every luxury travel advisor in the world is in one building for 4 days. LINX missing ILTM 2026 entirely means another year of competitor positioning without pushback.

## Attendance Options (Ranked by Feasibility)

### Option A — Trade Visitor (FREE, Available Now)
Register as a trade professional. No pre-scheduled buyer meetings, but full floor access. Walk the show, attend education sessions, meet exhibitors in their booths. Best for intelligence gathering and opportunistic networking.

**Cost:** Free registration. Travel + accommodation est. €1,500–3,500 depending on dates.
**Timeline to execute:** Book now. Cannes hotels for ILTM week sell out in October–November.

### Option B — Media/Press Pass
If Stein Studios or LINX has any media credential (blog, newsletter, podcast), apply for press accreditation. Gives full access + press briefings with major operators.

**Cost:** Free (if granted). Same travel/accommodation as above.
**Timeline to execute:** Apply immediately at iltm.com.

### Option C — Buyer Participation
ILTM buyers are pre-vetted luxury travel buyers (tour operators, advisors, DMCs). If LINX can position itself as a qualified buyer of operator inventory (which it is, via its network), buyer participation gives access to the pre-scheduled meeting system.

**Cost:** Invitation-only. Contact ILTM buyer team.
**Timeline to execute:** Contact now — buyer qualification takes time and the 2026 edition is in 22 days (unlikely to get in this year, but establish the relationship for 2027).

### Option D — Exhibitor (TIER 1 presence, 2027 target)
ILTM exhibitors start at ~EUR8,000–15,000 for the smallest space. This is a 2027 target, not a 2026 option given the timeline.

**Action:** Submit exhibitor inquiry NOW to get on the 2027 exhibitor list.
