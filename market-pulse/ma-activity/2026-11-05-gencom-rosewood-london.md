**WHEN:** Announced November 3–4, 2026. Transaction close date not separately confirmed; deal likely completed at or near announcement.

**WHERE:** Central London, UK. High Holborn, within walking distance of legal/financial district, Covent Garden, City of London.

**WHY:** Gencom's first European luxury hotel acquisition — expanding out of US/Caribbean. Capital investment program planned = repositioning / renovation post-acquisition. White Bridge Capital partnership strengthening with second deal.

**HOW:** New luxury hotel ownership = distribution strategy review window.
