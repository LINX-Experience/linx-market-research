---

## Why It Happened

**Goods luxury headwinds:**
- Chinese luxury demand softness (post-COVID hangover, anti-corruption campaign extension, domestic brand nationalism)
- Gen Z luxury fatigue (backlash against conspicuous consumption branding)
- Tariff impacts on cross-border luxury goods pricing
- Resale market disruption (The RealReal, Vestiaire Collective reducing demand for new)

**Experiential luxury tailwinds:**
- Cannot be resold, replicated, or discounted
- Experiences become identity / social capital (not ownership)
- UHNW demographic: privacy + access > ownership + display
- Post-pandemic "time is the new luxury" — experience scarcity is irreproducible
- Wellness + expedition + multigenerational = fastest-growing UHNW categories (Virtuoso +28–44% wellness YoY)
