## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer + Innovator (explosive growth mandate; Rodriguez is driving aggressive expansion and all-suite repositioning; LINX represents a novel high-quality demand channel)

**Key motivation:** Atlas is in a hypergrowth phase (+22% 2025, +49% H1 2026). They need high-quality UHNW demand to fill an expanding fleet (Atlas Adventurer 2028 adds ~40% capacity). Virtuoso advisors are their primary channel — LINX offers a complementary UHNW peer-trust channel with lower commission cost. For Rodriguez and Anderson (EVP Sales), the pitch is: "LINX members who've sailed Atlas are the highest-converting ambassadors you don't yet compensate."

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We have Virtuoso — that's our UHNW channel."* — LINX answer: Virtuoso advisors recommend; LINX members who've sailed with you recommend. Different trust signal, same customer. Peer-trust converts the UHNW traveler who researches through their own network before they ever call an advisor.
2. *"Our commission structure is locked with Virtuoso."* — LINX at 10% is below, not competitive with, Virtuoso. Not a conflict; it's a parallel channel for direct peer-referred bookings.
3. *"We're growing fast — do we need another distribution channel?"* — Atlas Adventurer adds 40% fleet capacity in 2028. Opening-year demand for a new ship requires all channels. LINX's UHNW peer-trust network is the highest-margin new demand source.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Kristian C. Anderson (EVP Global Sales) directly. Frame as a Virtuoso-complementary UHNW peer-trust channel with lower commission overhead. Use the Atlas Adventurer (2028) as the hook: "Let's build the LINX referral base now so your 2028 capacity has a waitlist of UHNW travelers whose trusted friends sailed with you."

**Intro path:** Cold. Expedition cruise industry contacts, ILTM Cannes if Atlas exhibits, or Florida travel industry connections.
