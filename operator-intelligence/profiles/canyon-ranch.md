## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer (VICI investment = expansion mandate; Austin + future properties need distribution for opening years)

**Key motivation:** New properties (Austin Oct 2026; future Fort Worth, Houston announced) need opening-year distribution partners. Canyon Ranch's standard Virtuoso + GDS channels cover existing properties; new locations need incremental UHNW distribution. LINX's peer trust model is highest-value for opening-year windows when no established advisor relationships exist.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We have Virtuoso and GDS — distribution is covered."* — LINX answer: Virtuoso advisors recommend; LINX members who've actually stayed there recommend. These are architecturally different. The peer-trust signal converts UHNW travelers who don't use advisors.
2. *"Our marketing budget is managed centrally — partnership decisions require HQ sign-off."* — Need Mark Rivers (CEO) or CMO-level introduction, not property sales team.
3. *"Our commission structure is already established with Virtuoso — we can't have parallel channels at different rates."* — LINX rate (10%) is at or below Virtuoso override rates; not a conflict.

**Recommended approach:** Target Austin property specifically (Oct 2026 opening = maximum receptivity). Frame LINX as opening-year demand-gen for UHNW Texas-based travelers. Intro path: Mark Rivers (CEO) via wellness industry contact or VICI Properties investor relations crossover.

**Intro path:** Cold. Canyon Ranch has a Leisure & Wellness Business Development contact (Kelsey Massee-Perone) for West/Central US; this is the entry point for initial contact before escalating to executive level.
