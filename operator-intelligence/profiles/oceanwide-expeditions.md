## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Innovator + Revenue Optimizer

New CEO Rémi Bouysset (June 2025) is building North American distribution from scratch via VacationPort. He is in distribution-building mode — 30+ years experience means he has seen every model and is evaluating what works. The VacationPort announcement language ("significantly expanding reach and visibility") signals a growth-stage operator looking for new channels.

**Key motivation:** Expand North American bookings. Rémi Bouysset is building a new distribution strategy — LINX is an early-mover opportunity with zero friction cost (no tech integration required, referral-first).

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We just launched VacationPort — we're focused on that."* Counter: VacationPort is advisor-driven; LINX is peer-trust-driven. These are complementary, not competing. VacationPort puts you in front of advisors; LINX puts you in front of the UHNW traveler who has BEEN on a Weddell Sea voyage.
2. *"Our passengers are adventure-oriented, not typical UHNW."* Counter: Weddell Sea record voyages and Antarctic deep-ice access ARE UHNW-tier experiences. The overlap between expedition customers and LINX's trust graph is highest in polar travel.
3. *"We're a Dutch company — not focused on US referral networks."* Counter: VacationPort announcement explicitly names US and Canada as the growth priority. LINX's trust graph starts in the US and extends globally.

**Recommended approach:** Warm intro via VacationPort relationship manager (Oct 2025 partnership = active contact). Or direct to Rémi Bouysset on LinkedIn — new CEO, distribution-building mandate, receptive to new channel proposals.

**Intro path:** VacationPort contact intermediary OR cold LinkedIn to Rémi Bouysset.
