**Overview:** Inkaterra is Peru's most iconic eco-luxury hotel group, founded in 1975 by José Koechlin von Stein when he built what became the first eco-lodge in Latin America at Machu Picchu. The company pioneered sustainable luxury in South America and operates a fully vertical model: hotels, conservation research stations, guided naturalist experiences, and the Inkaterra Asociación (IIA) conservation non-profit. Inkaterra's hotels are Virtuoso partners (confirmed at Machu Picchu Pueblo Hotel and Hacienda Urubamba on Virtuoso.com), with La Casona topping Condé Nast Traveller Gold List 2026. Koechlin was nominated for a 2026 Virtuoso Sustainability Award. 200,000+ travelers hosted per year (2019 figure — current likely higher with 7 properties).

**Key distinction:** 50-year conservation legacy, founder still active as CEO. Inkaterra has no competitor for its specific vertical (conservation + luxury + Peru heritage + Amazon access under one brand). The addition of Cabo Blanco (northern coast, 2026) adds a new geography. Virtuoso confirmation is the strongest channel signal in this profile.

CONFIDENCE: Founder/CEO CONFIRMED (inkaterra.com). Virtuoso membership CONFIRMED (virtuoso.com two property listings). La Casona CNT Gold List 2026 CONFIRMED (inkaterra.com newsletter). Revenue UNVERIFIED.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (Koechlin has built Peru's most famous eco-luxury brand over 50 years; conservation and community are foundational, not marketing; he has received AACSB Influential Leader recognition, LEC Award, HOLA Lifetime Achievement — this is a legacy builder)

**Key motivation:** Conservation funding through tourism; building Peru's global luxury reputation; protecting Machu Picchu (Koechlin has publicly spoken about making Machu Picchu carbon-neutral); growing the portfolio to new geographies (Cabo Blanco 2026 = northern coast/surf).

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We've been operating for 50 years. Our Virtuoso and agent relationships are deep and established."*
   Response: LINX operates upstream of Virtuoso — the peer recommendation that says "go to Peru, stay at Inkaterra, not the Belmond" comes before the Virtuoso conversation. LINX is the trust signal that channels UHNW travelers to Inkaterra vs. competitors.
2. *"We receive 200,000+ travelers per year. Distribution isn't our problem."*
   Response: Quality of traveler matters more than quantity at Inkaterra's price point. LINX brings peer-trusted UHNW travelers who convert to multi-property stays and generate more Inkaterra referrals.
3. *"We have an active Virtuoso sustainability awards nomination and conservation brand — we don't need help with our story."*
   Response: LINX amplifies that story through UHNW peer networks. A conservation-curious billionaire asking their peer "where should I stay to understand Amazonian conservation" needs to reach Inkaterra first — LINX is that routing mechanism.

**Recommended approach:** Contact José Koechlin or Inkaterra's sales/distribution team (sales@inkaterra.com). Lead with the conservation peer-recommendation story — "the person who can explain why Inkaterra matters to their UHNW peer is worth more than any Virtuoso advisor placement." Given Koechlin's 2026 Virtuoso Sustainability Award nomination, connecting via Virtuoso Travel Week (Las Vegas, August 2026) would have been ideal. Post-award period (post-Aug 2026) is now the entry window — congratulations outreach opens the conversation.

**Intro path:** LATA (Latin American Travel Association) or Virtuoso network connection. Inkaterra's Cabo Blanco opening (new property) is a natural outreach moment for new partnership conversations.
