**Property Overview:** Mashpi Lodge sits in a 1,300-hectare private cloud forest reserve in Ecuador's Chocó Andino — one of the world's most biodiverse ecosystems and a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. The lodge operates as a "biology laboratory for guests," featuring an aerial tram, Sky Bike, hummingbird garden, and ongoing scientific research with international universities. 22 rooms (panoramic floor-to-ceiling windows; full board; guided activities included). Operated by Metropolitan Touring Ecuador, the country's largest and oldest tour operator (founded 1953), which also runs Galápagos expedition yachts and Casa Gangotena boutique hotel in Quito.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Relationship Builder (Metropolitan Touring has been building agent and conservation relationships for 70+ years; Mashpi Lodge is their flagship luxury statement)

**Key Motivation:** Metropolitan Touring is Ecuador's most established tourism company and Roque Sevilla is a prominent conservationist (former mayor, environmentalist, international NGO leader). Mashpi Lodge is both a commercial asset and a conservation statement — the reserve protects critically endangered Chocó Andino habitat. The company's 2025 Sustainability Report confirms ongoing measurable impact metrics. LINX peer referral resonates as a "trusted recommendation from someone who experienced the conservation mission" — not just a hotel stay.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "We already have strong Virtuoso and direct agent distribution." → Counter: LINX brings peer-referred UHNW who come specifically because a trusted friend described the science and conservation experience — these guests are higher-margin, longer-stay, and more likely to return.
2. "Metropolitan Touring has 70 years of agent relationships — another distribution platform adds complexity." → Counter: LINX isn't a distribution platform in the traditional sense. It's peer attribution infrastructure that sits alongside your existing channels and captures the word-of-mouth you can't currently measure.
3. "Ecuador is a niche destination — LINX's UHNW network may not prioritize it." → Counter: La Liste Top Hotels 2026 (95 pts), Two Michelin Keys, cloud forest is #1 trending biome for 2027 (rich guest validation data exists). The guests who discover Mashpi via peer recommendation self-select as high-conviction, high-value travelers.

**Recommended Approach:** Engage Metropolitan Touring's sales team (not property-level). Lead with the La Liste 95 / Michelin Keys as validation that Mashpi is a global top-tier property ready for LINX. Frame LINX as a tool to extend Metropolitan Touring's US and European HNW demand beyond its existing Virtuoso advisor network. The conservation angle is secondary (trust first, mission second).

**Intro Path:** Virtuoso's SDR team (Metropolitan Touring is a Virtuoso partner — Stein may be able to get a warm intro through Virtuoso's CDO Thatcher Brown or through Virtuoso's Latin America team). Adventure Smith Explorations (US-based specialist that features Mashpi). Cold outreach to dpdecuador@metropolitan-touring.com.
