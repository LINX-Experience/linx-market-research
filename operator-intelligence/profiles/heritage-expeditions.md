**Overview:** Heritage Expeditions is New Zealand's oldest and most established small-ship expedition operator, founded by biologist Rodney Russ in 1984–85. Now run by his sons Aaron and Nathan, it operates three ships with an emphasis on natural history, science, and conservation. The company is known for access to rarely-visited wildlife destinations (NZ subantarctic islands, Ross Sea, Pacific, Sub-Antarctic South Georgia) with onboard scientists and naturalists. Heritage Discoverer (formerly Silver Explorer, ex-Silversea ship) debuts its maiden season in 2027–28, adding Europe/Atlantic itineraries.

**Key distinction:** The only expedition operator based in New Zealand, with exclusive or near-exclusive access to the NZ subantarctic island group (UNESCO World Heritage Sites with strict permit limits). The Heritage Discoverer expansion into Atlantic/European routes in 2027–28 marks a significant fleet growth moment.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary / Relationship Builder (Aaron and Nathan Russ inherited their father's scientific conservation ethos; decision-making is values-driven, not margin-driven; they actively lead expeditions)

**Key motivation:** Authentic expedition access to rarely-visited places; scientific rigor; NZ conservation legacy; building the family business into an international brand (Discoverer = major expansion).

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We're New Zealand-based and have a niche, loyal client base. We don't need a US platform."*
   Response: Heritage Discoverer's new Atlantic/European itineraries in 2027–28 target the exact UHNW North American and European travelers who rely on peer trust for first-time expedition booking. LINX is the trust infrastructure for that expansion.
2. *"Our guests are naturalists and scientists, not luxury travel consumers."*
   Response: The overlap between UHNW expedition travelers and LINX's ICP is significant — the peer recommendation from a trusted friend who completed the Ross Sea or NZ Subantarctic voyage is worth more than any marketing.
3. *"We already use AdventureSmith and other specialist agents."*
   Response: LINX operates upstream — the peer recommendation that generates the first conversation with AdventureSmith is LINX's moment.

**Recommended approach:** Reach Aaron Russ via Heritage's commercial team. Lead with the Discoverer maiden season opportunity — a new ship, new routes, and a need to fill a larger capacity from a new geographic market is exactly when peer-trust distribution matters most.
