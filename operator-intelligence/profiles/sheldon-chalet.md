**The world's most remote luxury lodge.** Sheldon Chalet sits on a glacial nunatak (rock outcrop) at 6,000 feet in the Alaska Range, helicopter-access only, with a view of the Denali summit. Built on a dream of aviation pioneer and bush pilot Don Sheldon, who received a federal land patent in the 1970s. His children spent 15+ years permitting, then 3 years building. Opened February 2018 as Denali National Park's first (and by federal regulation, last) luxury hotel. No other facility will ever be permitted at this location. It is architecturally extraordinary — titanium and glass embedded in glacier rock, featured in Architect Magazine.

Rates: from **$2,300/person/night**; full-chalet buyout is the primary booking unit.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary — The Sheldon family built this to honor their father's legacy and create something genuinely irreplaceable. This is not a business first; it's a heritage project. Revenue is a means to sustain the vision, not the end goal.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting exclusivity and legacy while ensuring the right guests experience the chalet. They want guests who *deserve* the experience — people who understand wilderness and luxury together. LINX peer-verification aligns perfectly.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "We're fully booked through word-of-mouth; we don't need marketing."
2. "We're a family operation — we can't handle more demand than we have."
3. "Our guests are private; they don't want to be on a platform."

**Recommended Approach:** Do NOT frame as growth or demand generation. Frame as access control — LINX helps ensure only the right people reach them. The chalet has 10 beds; LINX members are exactly the person profile they'd want. Treat it as an exclusive access opportunity for LINX, not a sales pitch to Sheldon.

**Intro Path:** Ryan Sheldon is accessible via outdoor/adventure media channels. Alaska lodge community. Potential Stein Studios angle — expedition filmmaking in Denali is a credible door opener.
