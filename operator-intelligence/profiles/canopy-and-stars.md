## Company

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Founded | 2014 |
| Parent | Sawday's group (also owns Paws & Stay) |
| HQ | Bristol, UK |
| Properties | ~900 unique spaces in collection |
| Model | Curated glamping marketplace — NOT an asset owner |
| Vertical | Premium Glamping / Unique Stays |
| Geography | UK + Europe (primarily UK-centric) |
| Primary demographic | 26–45 (64%), female decision-maker (64%) |

**What Canopy & Stars is:** A curated booking marketplace for independently owned glamping and unique accommodation — treehouses, shepherd's huts, geodomes, safari tents, cabins, yurts. The company does NOT own properties; it curates a platform of ~900 owner-operators and charges them commission on bookings. The company is simultaneously a curation service, a media brand (1.6M+ OTS, £2M+ ad value in 2025), and a booking platform.

**What Canopy & Stars is NOT:** An asset owner, luxury expedition operator, or UHNW-category experience provider. The core market is UK glamping — accessible premium (£100–£500/night), not ultra-luxury (£1,000+/night).

**Revenue (UNVERIFIED):** No public figure. As a marketplace, revenue is commission from bookings (est. 15–22% per third-party sources, unverified). With ~900 properties at an estimated £200/night average, 65% occupancy, 60-day stays/year = ~£70M GMV. At 18% commission = ~£12.6M revenue. This is a rough estimate — treat as UNVERIFIED.
