## Business Model

Bluewater's self-positioning: "the only yachting company that unites all divisions in-house." Services span:
1. **Charter:** Central agency and retail charter brokerage (Mediterranean, Caribbean, global)
2. **Management:** Yacht management services on behalf of owners
3. **Crew Placement:** Global superyacht crew recruitment (a significant standalone revenue line)
4. **Crew Training:** MCA/STCW training courses — revenue-generating and differentiating
5. **Sales:** Yacht sales and new build consultation

The crew placement + training divisions make Bluewater structurally different from pure charter brokers. Crew placement relationships with owners are a direct pipeline to management and charter mandates.

The "only company that unites all divisions in-house" positioning reflects a founder who built each division intentionally.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting and expanding the owner relationship pipeline. Crew placement and management mandates create a captive charter book — protecting these relationships from poaching by larger brokers is the core strategic motivation.
