## Business Model

World Expeditions operates as an independent, Australian-owned adventure travel group with 50+ years in the market. The group spans 18 brands covering trekking, cycling, cultural, expedition, and comfort travel segments. Comfort tier offerings include "Everest High Passes in Comfort" and "Everest Base Camp Trek in Comfort" — indicating a step above budget adventure but not ultra-luxury positioning.

CEO Sue Badyari's 2025 positioning: travelers are "choosing depth over checklist-style trips" — consistent with the premium adventure shift but not a luxury pivot.

Owned eco-camps in the Himalaya and Australian Red Centre are the highest-differentiating assets — these are owned (not curated) experiences at remote luxury price points. Record early bookings reported for 2025 and 2026 departures.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary — 50-year independent company. Sue Badyari's public positioning around depth-over-checklist travel reflects genuine mission alignment rather than pure revenue optimization.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting the brand's independence and expertise positioning against larger adventure consolidators (Intrepid, G Adventures). LINX's peer-trust model (trust-verified recommendations, not volume-driven) aligns with World Expeditions' differentiation strategy.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. "We're a trekking company — our clients aren't UHNW."
   Response: "Your Everest Base Camp Comfort departures are $8K–$15K per person. That's not backpacker pricing. The traveler spending $12,000 on a Himalayan trek asks trusted friends 'is World Expeditions worth it?' LINX puts you in that conversation and attributes the referral."

2. "We have a strong direct booking culture — we don't need agents."
   Response: "LINX isn't an agent. It's peer attribution infrastructure. When a World Expeditions Himalayan veteran recommends you to their mountain-climbing network, LINX tracks that recommendation and rewards the referrer — without touching your direct booking flow."

3. "We're Australia-based — LINX is a US/Europe-focused network."
   Response: "UHNW adventure travel is global. Your owned Himalayan eco-camps are a genuine differentiator that travels well through peer-trust networks regardless of geography."

**Recommended Approach:** Conference outreach via Adventure Travel Trade Association (ATTA) Summit (annual) or direct to Sue Badyari. Lead with Himalayan eco-camp ownership as the peer-trust asset — "you own something that creates strong peer advocacy. LINX captures and compounds that advocacy."

**Intro Path:** No Stein connection found. ATTA Summit is best in-person channel. Cold outreach to Sydney HQ possible.
