Clayoquot Wilderness Lodge sits on 600 acres of old-growth temperate rainforest on the Bedwell River in Clayoquot Sound, a UNESCO Biosphere Reserve. Access is exclusively by scenic seaplane (45 minutes from Vancouver) or helicopter. The 20 tented pavilions position it as one of the world's premier glamping experiences — safari-style but in a boreal rainforest with Pacific wilderness activities (kayaking, surfing, horseback riding, bear watching).

KSL Capital Partners (private equity, known for Vail Resorts, various luxury hospitality assets) acquired in 2019. Baillie Lodges (Australian collection including Huka Lodge NZ, Longitude 131° Australia, Capella Lodge Lord Howe Island) took operational management in 2021 — marking Baillie's first international property. The brand received Three MICHELIN Keys in 2025 (second source; verify with Michelin).

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary → now PE-owned / Baillie-operated. The original founder story has been absorbed into the Baillie brand narrative. Current decision-maker is the Baillie Lodges leadership team (Australia-based) and KSL Capital's hospitality portfolio management.

**Key Motivation:** Maximizing occupancy during a 4-month season while protecting the ultra-exclusive brand positioning. The challenge: sell out 20 tents for 122 days without becoming accessible enough to dilute the brand.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. *"We're already sold out through Virtuoso and direct — we don't have capacity to allocate to another channel."*
   Response: "LINX doesn't require allocated inventory. We generate referrals — qualified UHNW travelers who contact you directly. If they're a fit, you take the booking. If you're full, the waitlist builds."

2. *"Our Baillie Lodges brand standards require partner vetting at the group level in Australia."*
   Response: "Understood. LINX is invitation-only — matching your positioning. We can structure an introduction through Baillie Lodges' Australian team, or pilot through the Canadian lodge directly."

3. *"Our season is short — operational focus during season, rest in off-season. Partner additions get deprioritized."*
   Response: "Perfect timing: approach in October–November (off-season). LINX setup requires nothing from your team during season operations."

**Recommended Approach:** Baillie Lodges leadership (Australian-based) for group buy-in. Lead with Pacific prestige positioning — Clayoquot fits the LINX 'adventure + ultra-luxury' narrative perfectly. Cite Michelin Keys as evidence the brand is LINX-tier. Approach via Virtuoso network or luxury lodge associations.

**Intro Path:** Baillie Lodges contact (Australia) → operational team at Clayoquot. Cold but accessible through luxury hospitality networks.
