## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (Urubshurow pioneered Mongolia tourism; legacy + authenticity are core brand values; cautious about anything that dilutes exclusivity)

**Key motivation:** Three Camel Lodge is a small, remote, deeply specialist property. With a new executive director (Bjorkman), 2025–2026 represents a moment of potential strategic refresh. LINX's value proposition: "Replace 20% specialist agent commissions with 10% LINX peer-referral on the guests who would have found you anyway through word-of-mouth in the UHNW expedition community."

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"Mongolia is a specialist destination — most guests come through expedition outfitters, not peer referrals."* — LINX answer: The UHNW traveler who has done Antarctica, Patagonia, and Svalbard is now looking at Mongolia. Those travelers talk to each other. LINX captures the peer recommendation that happens in the expedition community.
2. *"Our capacity is 17 gers — we don't need more demand."* — Use this as a yield argument: LINX's peer-trust referrals represent premium demand (willing to pay full rack rate; no price negotiation). Replace 1–2 agent-booked gers per season with LINX-referred gers and keep 5–10% more margin.
3. *"We're not ready for a technology partnership."* — LINX's integration requirement is minimal; operator simply confirms referral-originated bookings and pays commission on departure.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Kurt Bjorkman (new Executive Director) via LinkedIn or Nomadic Expeditions email. Frame as supplementing — not replacing — their existing agent channel. Use the expedition community angle: Pelorus, Cookson Adventures, Natural Habitat Adventures all have overlapping UHNW customers who do Mongolia.

**Intro path:** Cold but accessible. Mongolia/expedition travel community is small; possible introductions via Pelorus or Natural Habitat Adventures contacts.
