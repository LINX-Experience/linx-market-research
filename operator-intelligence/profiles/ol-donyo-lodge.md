**Overview:** Ol Donyo Lodge sits on the Mbirikani Ranch, a 1.6-million-acre community conservancy owned by 4,000 Maasai families, leased by Great Plains Conservation. The lodge offers 8 suites and 1 two-bedroom family suite, each positioned to maximize views of Mt. Kilimanjaro and Amboseli ecosystem wildlife. It became Kenya's first Relais & Châteaux member in 2013. The lodge focuses on private conservancy access — 500,000 acres of private Maasai land with zero other camps — combined with Great Plains Conservation's National Geographic-partnered conservation mission.

**Key distinction:** The lodge operates on private Maasai land as a community benefit model. 100% of the game-viewing area is exclusive to Ol Donyo guests. This is structurally different from national park lodges that share animal access with dozens of competitors.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (Dereck Joubert is a National Geographic Fellow, filmmaker, and conservation icon — LINX must speak mission, not margin)

**Key motivation:** Conservation funding, authentic wilderness access for UHNW guests, maintaining exclusivity and brand integrity. NOT commission optimization as a primary driver.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We already have Great Plains' full distribution through R&C and existing agents — why add another channel?"*
   Response: LINX is not a distribution channel. It is the trust layer above all channels — the peer recommendation that sends a UHNW guest to Great Plains BEFORE they engage any agent.
2. *"Our bookings are consistent. We don't have unsold inventory problems."*
   Response: LINX drives higher-value guests (avg booking 2x standard) with higher lifetime value. It's not about filling beds — it's about filling them with the right people.
3. *"We're not a technology company. How complex is this?"*
   Response: Zero operational complexity. LINX handles the trust layer. Great Plains continues booking normally — LINX just ensures your guests arrive with verified peer trust.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Dereck or Beverly Joubert directly. They respond to mission-aligned pitches; conservation + community benefit + UHNW access resonates. The "world's trust graph for extraordinary experiences" framing aligns with their National Geographic brand identity.

**Intro path:** Great Plains Conservation main office (Botswana HQ) or via R&C travel consultant network. Any National Geographic connection Stein has would be highest-value entry.
