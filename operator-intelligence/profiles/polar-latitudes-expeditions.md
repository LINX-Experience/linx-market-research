Polar Latitudes was founded in 2012 by John McKeon with a focus on citizen science integration on every polar voyage — the first expedition operator to do this. Albatros Expeditions, based in Copenhagen, was a Danish polar expedition company with separate ownership. In April 2025, the two companies announced a merger; the full operational merger to one entity completed in March 2026. The combined brand retains the Polar Latitudes name, with McKeon as Chairman and Patrick Shaw (former Quark Expeditions CEO) initially named CEO. Shaw died suddenly in 2026; Filip Werne Hermann (ferry/freight shipping background) was named CEO effective August 1, 2026.

**Leadership risk:** Two CEO transitions in under a year (merger → Shaw death → Hermann) creates organizational instability. Hermann has logistics/shipping background, not expedition experience. This is a material watch item for LINX.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (McKeon as Chairman) + Skeptic (new CEO Hermann)

The organization is in transition. McKeon built the brand on citizen science and mission-driven expedition; he's now a step removed from operations. Hermann is a logistics/operational executive with no expedition background. This creates a bifurcated audience: pitch McKeon on mission alignment (LINX validates peer trust in expedition), pitch Hermann on cost efficiency (LINX at 10% vs. 10–15% trade commission).

**Key Motivation:** Post-merger integration success. Proving the Albatros + Polar Latitudes combination can grow revenue without losing the citizen-science, small-ship identity.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. *"We're in the middle of a post-merger integration — not the right time for new distribution experiments."*
   Response: "Exactly why LINX works now. We don't require integration work — we send you qualified bookings. No new technology, no new processes."

2. *"Our adventure specialist agents are our lifeblood — Swoop, AdventureSmith, Adventure Life. We can't disrupt those relationships."*
   Response: "LINX serves a different buyer: UHNW travelers who don't use adventure travel agencies. We're additive, not competing."

3. *"Antarctic traveler acquisition is already highly efficient through word-of-mouth. Why do we need LINX?"*
   Response: "Exactly — word-of-mouth IS LINX. We just track it, attribute it, and pay you better than your advisors do per booking."

**Recommended Approach:** John McKeon (Chairman) is the entry point — expedition circuit presence, citizen science community. Lead with mission alignment: LINX peer-verification validates Antarctic experiences through the trust graph of people who've actually been. Commission pitch is secondary.

**Intro Path:** McKeon via LinkedIn, expedition cruise industry conferences, or through conservation/science community networks.
