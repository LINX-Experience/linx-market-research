## Business Model

Terres d'Aventure is France's oldest specialist adventure travel brand (founded 1976), operating under the Voyageurs du Monde group. The group positions itself as "market leader in France in tailor-made travel." Terres d'Aventure handles the adventure/trekking segment while Voyageurs du Monde serves the luxury tailor-made market.

**2026 Revenue Headwinds:** Voyageurs du Monde has explicitly stated it anticipates a decline in revenue and earnings in 2026 if Middle East hostilities continue. The group's Middle East exposure is material — adventure travel to Jordan, Morocco, Egypt, and other MENA destinations is a core Terres d'Aventure segment.

**Critical ICP Note:** As a division of a publicly traded French travel group, Terres d'Aventure faces structural obstacles to LINX adoption: (1) procurement decisions go through parent company; (2) group Kuoni/Voyageurs du Monde has existing technology vendor relationships; (3) French market operates under different advisor/commission structures than Anglo-American luxury travel.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer — as a publicly listed group brand, Terres d'Aventure's decisions are driven by parent company margin management. Middle East revenue headwinds in 2026 increase pressure to find new distribution channels.

**Key Motivation:** Recovering 2026 revenue shortfall from Middle East conflict impact. New channels (like LINX) would need to show near-term revenue contribution.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. "We serve a French market — LINX is an Anglophone luxury network."
   Response: "UHNW French travelers have international peer networks. Terres d'Aventure's brand recognition is a trust signal even for non-French buyers. LINX's trust graph is geography-agnostic."

2. "We're part of Voyageurs du Monde — decisions require group approval."
   Response: This is a legitimate structural barrier. LINX's best path to Terres d'Aventure is through Voyageurs du Monde group-level business development, not the brand directly.

3. "Our Middle East revenue is under pressure — not the time for new platform experiments."
   Response: "Revenue pressure is exactly when new channels matter. LINX's referral model costs nothing upfront — operators pay only when LINX drives a confirmed booking."

**Recommended Approach:** LOW priority for direct brand outreach. If pursuing, go through Voyageurs du Monde group-level partnerships. Best case: LINX signs a Voyageurs du Monde group deal that covers Terres d'Aventure + their flagship tailor-made business simultaneously. ILTM Cannes (Nov 30–Dec 3) would be the right venue for a Voyageurs du Monde group conversation.

**Intro Path:** No Stein connection found. ILTM Cannes exhibitor list check needed — does Voyageurs du Monde exhibit at ILTM?
