**COMO Shambhala Estate** is the wellness crown jewel of Christina Ong's COMO Hotels empire. Set on 22 private acres north of Ubud, Bali, with 31 suites and villas overlooking the Ayung River gorge. It operates 6 Wellness Paths (Cleanse, Nourish, Rest, Activate, Align, Heal). Day visitors can book individual treatments.

**La Liste 2026 rating: 90.5** — world-class. Condé Nast Traveler 2026 Readers' Choice: top destination spa. MICHELIN Key awarded.

Christina Ong, one of Singapore's most powerful businesswomen, oversees COMO alongside Club 21 and significant fashion retail. The group is closely held — no public financials.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Relationship Builder — Christina Ong built COMO on personal relationships with designers, architects, and chefs (Nobu, Bill Bensley, Aman architects). COMO Shambhala sells transformation, not transactions. Trust-based.

**Key Motivation:** Deepening COMO's wellness authority globally while maintaining ultra-luxury positioning. LINX peer recommendation is the highest-trust discovery channel available.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "COMO already has an established global distribution network — we don't need another layer."
2. "Our guests are private; a referral platform creates visibility risk."
3. "Our decision-making is centralized at Singapore; local property staff cannot commit."

**Recommended Approach:** Approach via the Bali property GM or through COMO Group's COO (Wortelboer) rather than trying to reach Christina Ong directly. Frame LINX as complementary to Preferred/Virtuoso — a trust-layer that reaches guests who bypass traditional advisor channels. Specifically valuable for the retreat category, where peer testimony drives more decisions than advisor commissions.

**Intro Path:** Singapore luxury hospitality network. ILTM Cannes (COMO attends). Virtuoso Travel Week (if COMO is a Virtuoso partner). Expedition/wellness media contacts.
