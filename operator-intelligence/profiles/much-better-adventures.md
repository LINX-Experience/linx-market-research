## Business Model

Much Better Adventures operates as a curated adventure travel marketplace/booking platform, with a "5% of revenues to conservation projects" mission. The October 2026 wildlife small-group line launch signals a move toward nature/wildlife positioning. Business model is primarily B2C direct booking (online), not advisor-dependent.

The Gresham House Ventures £5M investment (Dec 2024) confirms VC backing and a growth mandate. Gresham House specializes in sustainable/impact investing — funding rationale is aligned with MBA's conservation mission rather than pure travel growth.

**Key positioning gap for LINX:** MBA is NOT luxury. Its value proposition is sustainable, accessible adventure at affordable price points — a different market segment from LINX's UHNW peer-trust network.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Innovator — VC-backed startup with a sustainability mission. Leadership team (unknown by name) is likely building toward acquisition or B2B white-label model based on Gresham House growth mandate.

**Key Motivation:** Growing the customer base and conservation mission simultaneously. VC investors want growth metrics. LINX's peer-trust model could theoretically accelerate word-of-mouth for new products, but MBA's direct booking DNA is a structural mismatch.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. "Our customers are adventure seekers on a budget — not UHNW travelers."
   Response: "LINX is not for your current customers. It's for the 5% of your audience who would pay 3x for a private departure with exclusive access — the wildlife trip that only 8 people in the world have done. Build the premium tier first; we give it peer-trust distribution."

2. "We're a platform company — LINX is a commission layer we don't need."
   Response: This objection is legitimate. MBA's business model is B2C direct booking with no advisor layer. LINX doesn't add value for direct-booking platforms without a premium tier.

3. "We're focused on conservation impact, not revenue growth."
   Response: "LINX's commission structure donates to operator-designated conservation causes. 5% of LINX-driven revenue could flow back to your World Land Trust partnership." (Speculative — verify LINX's impact-giving capabilities before using this pitch.)

**Recommended Approach:** LOW priority. If LINX builds a premium/private departure tier for adventure operators, re-engage Much Better Adventures. Current ICP mismatch: direct booking, low price point, no luxury positioning.

**Intro Path:** Cold outreach via London adventure travel community. No path identified.
