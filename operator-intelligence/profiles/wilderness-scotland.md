## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer (PE-backed, growth-mode)

Mobeus investment (2018) + Craig Burton as CEO signals a business optimizing for scale and returns. The Virtuoso Preferred deal (Jan 2024) is part of a systematic distribution buildout — these are not relationship-driven decisions, they're channel optimization decisions.

**Key motivation:** Maximize distribution ROI. For a PE-backed operator at £142M revenue, the LINX pitch must be ROI-first: "How many UHNW bookings does LINX generate vs. the cost?"

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We're too large for a startup referral network."* Counter: LINX's target is not volume — it's the highest-value bookings. Even 5 UHNW referrals at £15,000 each is £75,000 in LINX-attributable bookings, with zero marketing spend.
2. *"Our customers are adventure travelers, not typical UHNW."* Counter: The National Geographic "Europe's No. 1" designation attracts UHNW travelers specifically — the peer-trust signal for Scotland expeditions is extremely high among UHNW who have done a Wilderness Scotland private trip.
3. *"We already have Virtuoso — we don't need another layer."* Counter: Same response as other Virtuoso-partnered operators: LINX adds the peer layer above Virtuoso advisors.

**Recommended approach:** Low priority at current state. If MV Luxury Travel generates a UHNW private voyage product, revisit. Otherwise, deprioritize in favor of higher-ICP operators.

**Intro path:** Cold outreach to Craig Burton (CEO, Active Travel Group) or via Pure Life Experiences intermediary.
