## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (Zita Cobb built Fogo Island Inn as a vehicle for community economic resilience, not personal wealth — the strongest possible founder-mission alignment)

**Key Motivation:** Zita Cobb's mission is explicitly not hotel profitability — it's the economic revitalization of a fishing community left behind by globalization. Shorefast is her vehicle; the inn is the revenue engine. She wrote/speaks extensively about "reciprocal economies" and the duty of business to communities. LINX peer recommendation resonates because it represents something Virtuoso cannot: authentic human endorsement from real travelers who chose Fogo Island not because an advisor upsold them, but because a friend said it changed their life.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "We're a charity — adding a commission layer conflicts with our model." → Counter: LINX peer referrers earn nothing. They refer because the experience was transformational. LINX attributes that referral and pays the operator less, not more — the 10% goes to building the trust network, not to individual referrers.
2. "We're already well-distributed through Virtuoso and R&C." → Counter: Virtuoso and R&C send travelers who have been sold by advisors. LINX sends travelers who were told by a trusted friend. The peer-referred guest arrives different — more prepared, more committed, more likely to return and refer again.
3. "We're selective about who represents our experience." → Counter: LINX's selectivity is structural — only verified UHNW who have experienced the inn can refer it. That's tighter than any Virtuoso advisor program.

**Recommended Approach:** Engage Zita Cobb directly on the "reciprocal economy" angle. LINX peer referral creates economic reciprocity at scale: trusted peers vouching for Fogo Island builds community economic value more authentically than any advisor relationship. Reference her Skift interview ("How to Fix Travel") — LINX is part of that fix. Lead with mission alignment, not commission savings.

**Intro Path:** Skift/PhocusWire speaker circuit (Zita is a frequent speaker). Relais & Châteaux network. Cold email to inn directly (small team, Zita likely accessible). ILTM Cannes Nov 30 — high probability attendance for a Michelin-rated R&C member.
