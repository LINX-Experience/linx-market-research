## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary (85-year family legacy; brand is the family identity; growth must feel authentic to the founding mission)

**Key motivation:** Brightwood is protecting an 85-year brand. The residential development signals ambition for growth, but only on the ranch's own terms. LINX's value proposition must be framed as: "Peer trust is the purest expression of your word-of-mouth business model — LINX is simply the infrastructure that makes those referrals trackable and attributable."

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We've operated on referral for 85 years without a platform."* — True; but untracked referrals are invisible and uncompensated. LINX makes the referral graph visible and rewards the people already driving business.
2. *"Our guests don't want to feel like they're in a commercial referral network."* — LINX's trust-network framing (not affiliate marketing) addresses this directly.
3. *"The Saturday-to-Saturday format means capacity is fixed — additional referrals just create a waitlist."* — Use this as a positive: LINX builds the waitlist of UHNW guests who want the experience, increasing yield and validating the residential development.

**Recommended approach:** Direct outreach to Sarah Livia Brightwood or GM Eliezer Quiñones framing LINX as the digital infrastructure for their existing referral culture. ILTM Cannes if they exhibit (not confirmed). Wellness media context is the right entry point — Health + wellness travel press is their world.

**Intro path:** Cold. No known Stein Retzlaff connections to Brightwood family.
