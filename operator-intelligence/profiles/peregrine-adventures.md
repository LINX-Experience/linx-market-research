## Critical ICP Assessment

**Peregrine Adventures is not a LINX target operator.** The disqualifying factors are:

1. **Sub-brand of a $809M parent:** Intrepid Group was profiled at 50/100 as too large and mass-market. Peregrine is a smaller sub-brand within that group — the parent decision-making structure makes individual brand outreach ineffective without group approval.

2. **50+ demographic, NOT UHNW:** Peregrine explicitly targets travelers 50+ with "plenty of creature comforts of home" (3–4 star accommodations). This is accessible adventure for older travelers, not ultra-luxury for UHNW buyers.

3. **Mid-range pricing:** $4,985–$10,938 is at the lower end of the peer-trust price threshold. The UHNW traveler spending $5K on a 50+ adventure tour is not the LINX archetype.

4. **B2C direct + agent mix:** Intrepid Group's core distribution is a mix of direct booking and standard agent commissions — no Virtuoso, no high-commission advisor relationship that LINX would displace.
