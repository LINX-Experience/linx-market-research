**Eleven Experience** builds destination lodges in extreme terrain — glacier valleys, arctic tundra, backcountry snowfields. The founding thesis: combine best-in-class wilderness access with uncompromised luxury hospitality. Deplar Farm sits on 1.8 million acres of the Troll Peninsula in northern Iceland, offering heli-skiing with 3,000-foot vertical runs and fly fishing on the Huséyjarkvisl River. Consistently ranked among the world's best adventure lodges: Condé Nast Traveler Gold List, Robb Report "Best Hotels in the World." Corporate retreat bookings (full-lodge buyouts) are an explicit revenue line.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary — The lodge product is built around an extraordinary founder vision of combining wilderness extremes with five-star luxury. Brand identity = everything.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting brand exclusivity while growing demand without overcrowding. LINX delivers exactly what they need: quality-verified, UHNW demand from trusted peer networks, not mass-market travelers.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "Our guests find us through the best advisors already — why add another layer?"
2. "We're small. 80 guests a season at Deplar Farm. We can't absorb much more volume."
3. "Will LINX members understand what we do or expect a typical resort?"

**Recommended Approach:** Position LINX as access to a client profile they don't currently reach — the ultra-networked entrepreneur or family office principal who discovers lodges through peer recommendation, not travel agents. Emphasize invite-only community credibility. Limited-capacity properties are IDEAL for LINX's low-volume, high-value model.

**Intro Path:** Cold outreach via adventure press (Robb Report, CNT Traveler connection) or through a mutual alpine / expedition contact. Eleven shows up at luxury adventure trade (ILTM, Virtuoso Travel Week). Monaco GP connection unlikely — this is a ski/expeditions brand, not a yachting crowd.
