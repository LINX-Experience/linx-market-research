**Overview:** Lamai Serengeti is Nomad Tanzania's flagship Serengeti camp, positioned in the extreme northern Serengeti (Lamai Wedge) for exclusive access to the Great Migration's Mara River crossings. The camp runs 12 tents: 8 in the main lodge plus 4-tent Mkombe's House (bookable exclusively). It operates year-round as a permanent camp (not seasonal) with the Kogakuria Kopjes providing year-round wildlife. The northern Serengeti location provides the most dramatic wildebeest river crossings from July–October. Owned and operated by Nomad Tanzania, with the Nomad Trust conservation funding model.

**Key distinction:** Lamai's northern position puts it closer to the Mara River crossings than any other Serengeti camp. The 4-tent exclusive Mkombe's House (private camp within a camp) is a LINX-ready product: entire camp buyout for UHNW families, priced at premium.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer (Nomad Tanzania's business model is margin-focused; they compete hard on quality-to-price vs. mega-operators like Singita/Wilderness)

**Key motivation:** The Great Migration is a commodity product among dozens of Serengeti operators. Nomad Tanzania differentiates on guide quality and camp character. LINX peer trust = differentiation signal from verified UHNW guests.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We work with the top safari outfitters already. We're at capacity in peak season."*
   Response: LINX drives off-peak and shoulder season bookings through peer recommendation from guests whose trust network includes other UHNW travelers planning similar trips at different times.
2. *"Why would we reduce commissions paid to our best relationships?"*
   Response: LINX operates alongside existing agent relationships — it creates a new incremental channel of direct peer-trust bookings that do not originate from agents.
3. *"We've operated for 30+ years. We know our distribution."*
   Response: LINX is for the next 30 years — UHNW travelers are shifting to peer trust as their primary discovery mechanism.

**Recommended approach:** Approach Amy Ramsay or Nomad Tanzania's director of sales. Conservation mission alignment (Nomad Trust) is a secondary hook. Lead with the differentiation opportunity in a crowded Serengeti market.

**Intro path:** Cold outreach via Nomad Tanzania's commercial contact. Any We Are Africa or Connections Barcelona relationship would provide a 1-degree path.
