## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Relationship Builder — Monaco superyacht boutique lives and dies on personal relationships. Commission structure and repeat client base are the core business model.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting existing client relationships while growing charter volume. LINX's peer-trust attribution solves a core pain: when a friend of a client books a charter, TWW currently has no way to attribute or reward that referral.

**Top 3 Objections:**

1. "Our business is relationships — we don't need a platform."
   Response: "LINX is not a platform your clients see. It's attribution infrastructure on the back end. When a client's friend books because of a recommendation, LINX tells you it happened and rewards the referrer — without exposing your client relationships to any marketplace."

2. "We already work with sub-brokers."
   Response: "Sub-brokers charge 10–15%. The traveler who referred a charter to you earned nothing. LINX rewards the referrer at 5% (yacht rate), saves you 5–10 points vs. a sub-broker, and attributable referrals compound over time."

3. "Monaco relationships don't work through apps."
   Response: "LINX is invitation-only and operates through direct relationship channels — the same WhatsApp/Signal chains your clients already use. No app downloads required for the referrer. It's attribution, not a booking engine."

How much of that came from a client's recommendation that you never tracked and never compensated? LINX solves that in 48 hours."
