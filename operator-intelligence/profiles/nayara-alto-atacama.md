## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Founder-Visionary

Leo Ghitis built Nayara from a single Costa Rica property to a multi-country ultra-luxury brand — 3 Michelin Keys at Nayara Springs is a founder who cares deeply about quality and recognition. The Atacama expansion (partnered with Tánica) shows deliberate international growth rather than rapid scaling.

**Key motivation:** Brand stewardship and quality distribution. Ghitis will respond to: "LINX puts Nayara in the trust graph of UHNW travelers whose peers have STAYED at your properties — not just seen them on a list."

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We have LHW and travel agents covering our distribution."* Counter: LHW gives you listing and agent access. LINX gives you the layer above: the UHNW traveler whose close friend actually stayed at Nayara Alto Atacama and can speak to the experience. That referral is worth $20K+.
2. *"Our Costa Rica properties are the flagship — Atacama is a newer addition."* Counter: The Atacama is precisely where peer trust is most valuable — a destination travelers are less familiar with needs a trust signal more than Arenal does. LINX closes that gap.
3. *"We've built our brand on editorial recognition (Michelin, CNT, T+L) — why do we need referrals?"* Counter: Editorial recognition drives aspiration. Peer referral drives conversion. The gap between "I want to go to Nayara" and "I'm booking Nayara" is closed by a trusted peer who has been.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Nayara via LHW relationship manager or directly via their trade/agent contacts. The 2025 Michelin Keys announcement (October 2025) is a strong conversation opener: "Congratulations on the first-ever 3 Michelin Keys in Costa Rica. LINX is building the peer-trust channel for exactly this tier of operator."

**Intro path:** Cold outreach. Possible warm entry via LHW (The Leading Hotels of the World) relationship if any LINX advisor has an LHW contact. Alternatively, journey Latin America or Lightfoot Travel may know the Atacama property team.
