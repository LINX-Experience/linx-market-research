**B-Corp:** Re-certified 2025. Active sustainability positioning.

**REI partnership:** From January 2026, Exodus operates the REI Adventures relaunch under the REI brand — white-label product for REI Co-op members. This confirms mass-market volume alongside Signature Collection premium positioning.

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer (Travelopia/KKR mandate = margin improvement and channel diversification)

**Key motivation:** KKR ownership requires profitable growth and channel efficiency. Sasha Andrews's public statements signal premium tier is a priority growth area. LINX offers incremental distribution in UHNW referral network — but only if the ticket to access the target demographic is UHNW-verified referrals rather than mass-market agents.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We already have an agent network — what does LINX add?"* — LINX answer: UHNW peer referrals are qualitatively different from agency bookings; they drive higher-ticket Signature Collection bookings specifically.
2. *"We're part of Travelopia — partnership decisions aren't local."* — Corporate gate is a real obstacle; needs regional or UK commercial team buy-in first.
3. *"Our commission structure is already competitive."* — Arbitrage argument doesn't land; must lead with referral quality and UHNW profile.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Sasha Andrews (Director of Industry Sales) directly at the Signature Collection level. Frame LINX as a premium demand-gen channel for Signature Collection specifically, not Exodus-wide. Use ILTM Cannes if Exodus exhibits (not confirmed).

**Intro path:** Cold outreach via LinkedIn or trade event. No known Stein Retzlaff connections to Travelopia.
