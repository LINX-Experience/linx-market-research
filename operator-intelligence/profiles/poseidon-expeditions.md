**Overview:** Poseidon Expeditions is a privately-held, single-ship polar expedition operator founded in 1999 by Angelica Vorea. Known for a deliberate single-ship strategy (recently reversed with a newbuild), Poseidon operates the 114-passenger MV Sea Spirit on year-round polar itineraries — Arctic in summer, Antarctica in winter/spring, with British Isles and Greenland shoulder-season itineraries. CEO Vorea has described the single-ship model as enabling close involvement in every guest experience from expedition leadership to onboard service. The company has been publicly active on early-bird discount strategy (25% for 2027–28 advance bookings) and added an all-inclusive beverage package in 2026.

**Key distinction:** 25+ year polar expedition specialist with the same founder-CEO since inception. Vorea's female-founder narrative and long-term relationship-building approach creates a Relationship Builder archetype unusual in the expedition cruise market.

CONFIDENCE: CEO/founder CONFIRMED (press releases, Cruise Industry News). Fleet details CONFIRMED (poseidonexpeditions.com, Wikipedia). Revenue UNVERIFIED. Newbuild confirmed (CIN, Seatrade Cruise).

## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Relationship Builder (Vorea has led the same company for 25+ years; she speaks about personal involvement in guest experience; this is a founder who wants to know who her guests are)

**Key motivation:** Building long-term guest relationships; filling the ship with the right travelers for the expedition experience; protecting the small-group, authentic-expedition model while growing via the newbuild.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We've operated profitably for 25 years on direct and agent mix. Why change distribution?"*
   Response: The newbuild changes the math — more capacity requires more qualified leads from new channels. LINX provides peer-trust leads that convert at higher rates than cold acquisition.
2. *"We're a small Cyprus-based company. We don't have resources for new partnerships."*
   Response: LINX requires zero operational resources from Poseidon. It's a trust-layer above existing booking — 30 minutes to set up, then peer recommendations drive bookings through existing channels.
3. *"Our guests are polar specialists. Word-of-mouth is already how they find us."*
   Response: Exactly the LINX thesis. LINX formalizes and scales the word-of-mouth that's already working. The peer recommendation that currently happens informally between UHNW travelers can now be tracked, attributed, and rewarded.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Angelica Vorea directly. Lead with the newbuild expansion and the need to fill additional capacity from UHNW peer networks. Vorea's relationship-builder archetype responds to personal founder-to-founder outreach from Stein.

**Intro path:** Cold outreach via poseidonexpeditions.com press contacts. The polar expedition cruise community is small — any shared connection with polar expedition operators (Heritage Expeditions, Aurora, Swan Hellenic) would create a warm intro.
