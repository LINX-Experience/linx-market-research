## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer — Mahan and Tansey built the company around making more money through better service, not despite it. They are commercially pragmatic.

**Key Motivation:** Protecting broker revenue while differentiating on client trust and service quality. LINX aligns with the anti-churn, referral-first culture they already champion.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "We already have strong referrals — why pay even 5% to a referral network?"
2. "How does LINX verify referral attribution vs our existing client relationships?"
3. "Our charter clients are discreet — will LINX exposure conflict with client privacy?"

**Recommended Approach:** Frame LINX as incremental demand generation, not a replacement for their word-of-mouth model. Emphasize the ultra-verified UHNW buyer quality and that LINX operators receive referrals they'd never get cold. Mahan is likely the right door — he's US-based and leads US growth.

**Intro Path:** Boat International, Palm Beach yacht social circuit, or via a mutual Monaco contact. No direct Stein connection found. Cold but accessible via marine industry channels.
