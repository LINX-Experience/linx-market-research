## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer + Relationship Builder (dual)

The Beckons rebrand signals a PE-backed operator (KSL Capital) in active growth and integration mode. Michael Crawford as new CEO (May 2025) and Stephen Marshall as new VP Global Sales (Jan 2026) represent a team actively building out distribution. This is a team LOOKING for new, premium distribution channels — not entrenched in legacy models.

**Key motivation:** KSL Capital needs to demonstrate distribution excellence across 10 lodges on three continents. A portfolio-level LINX deal — covering the Chile properties and extending to Longitude 131°, Clayoquot, etc. — has higher strategic value than a single property conversation.

**Top 3 objections:**
1. *"We just rebranded and our distribution is in transition — not the right time."* Counter: The transition IS the right time — LINX fits cleanly into the new Beckons channel architecture before legacy contracts calcify.
2. *"We already have Virtuoso and specialist agents covering our properties."* Counter: LINX adds the trust layer above advisors — the UHNW traveler whose peer has STAYED at Tierra Patagonia vs. Torres del Paine alternatives. That's what Virtuoso can't provide.
3. *"Our Chile properties are specialist-dependent (Swoop, JLA) — we need regional depth."* Counter: LINX's referral graph is global, not regional. A UHNW traveler in London or New York deciding between Patagonia operators is exactly the peer-trust decision LINX solves.

**Recommended approach:** Contact Courtney Reagan (CCO) or Stephen Marshall (VP Global Sales) — both newly appointed and mandate-driven. Lead with the portfolio-level pitch: "Beckons has 10 lodges across 4 destinations. LINX puts all 10 in the trust graph of UHNW travelers whose peers have stayed at your properties."

**Intro path:** Cold outreach to stephen.marshall@beckons.com or via LinkedIn. ILTM Cannes (Nov 30–Dec 3, 2026) is a potential warm-entry point if Beckons exhibits — research exhibitor list.
