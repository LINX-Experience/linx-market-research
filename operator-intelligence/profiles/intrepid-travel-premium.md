## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer + Innovator (B-Corp mission + profitable growth mandate under Thornton)

**Why this score is honest:** Intrepid is too large, too mass-market, and too digitally direct to be a natural LINX operator. The Premium tier is growing but it is a tier within a mass-market adventure company, not an independent ultra-luxury brand. LINX's value proposition (trust network, 10% commission, UHNW referral access) doesn't create material change for an $809M operator.

**The only scenario where this works:** Intrepid's Premium tier develops a standalone brand identity and leadership team that can enter a partnership independently of the parent company. Not visible in 2026 data.

**Recommended approach:** Do not prioritize. If Intrepid's Premium tier spins out or is rebranded independently, re-profile. File and forget.
