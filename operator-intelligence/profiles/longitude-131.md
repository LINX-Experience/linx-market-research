**Property Overview:** Longitude 131° is Australia's most iconic outback luxury lodge, located within view of Uluru (Ayers Rock) in the Northern Territory. The lodge offers 16 safari-style tents on the desert dune top with unobstructed views of Uluru and Kata Tjuṯa. The property has operated under Beckons (formerly Baillie Lodges) since 2016. In March 2026, Baillie Lodges and Tierra Hotels unified under the new Beckons brand, backed by KSL Capital Partners with $100M+ invested across the portfolio. A new Beckons property near Uluru (9 suites) is planned for 2028. The 10th anniversary refurbishment (2024) invested $6M in the property. ADR starts from AUD ~$1,400/person/night inclusive.

**Beckons Rebrand:** Launched March 2026 as "a new luxury hospitality brand uniting the award-winning Baillie Lodges and Tierra Hotels portfolios." KSL acquired majority stake in Baillie Lodges in 2018; Tierra Hotels added to portfolio in 2022. Total: 9 locations, ~25 suites average per lodge, 4 continents. ## Psychology and Approach

**Archetype:** Revenue Optimizer (PE-backed, margin-focused post-rebrand)

**Key Motivation:** KSL Capital Partners has invested $100M+ across the Beckons portfolio and is building a scalable luxury lodge brand for eventual institutional sale/IPO. Distribution efficiency and revenue attribution are core to the investment thesis. Beckons' new CMO/marketing team (post-rebrand) is building cross-portfolio brand identity — LINX fits as a "trusted recommendation engine for ultra-HNW travelers" across all 9 properties.

**Top 3 Objections:**
1. "We already have strong distribution through LLoA and specialist agents" → Counter: LLoA tracks sales volume; LINX tracks trusted peer endorsement — the signal that closes the waitlisted UHNW booking.
2. "We're a PE-backed portfolio and can't do one-off arrangements" → Counter: Propose Beckons-wide preferred partner arrangement with consolidated reporting across all 9 lodges.
3. "The 10% rate is the same as direct savings we'd pass to guests" → Counter: LINX referral creates *new* demand from UHNW peer networks, not displacement of existing bookings.

**Recommended Approach:** Engage Beckons corporate sales/distribution team (not property-level). Lead with portfolio-level pitch: "LINX can deliver peer-referred UHNW bookings across all 9 Beckons properties from a single referral attribution dashboard." Reference the rebrand as an opportunity: "Beckons is building a new brand identity — LINX is the trust layer that makes it travel at UHNW word-of-mouth speed."

**Intro Path:** Luxury Lodges of Australia network connection (Stein likely has 2-degree access). Cold outreach to Beckons commercial team at beckons.com/trade or through trade media contacts.
